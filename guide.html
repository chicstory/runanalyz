💡 Aerobic Efficiency Factor (EF)
Formula: Speed (m/min) ÷ Average Heart Rate (bpm)
Analogous to a car's "fuel economy" (MPG), EF quantifies the physical distance propelled per single heartbeat over one minute.

Why Aerobic Efficiency (EF) Instead of Raw Pace?

Running pace fluctuates heavily due to grade variations, headwind, temperature, humidity, and acute fatigue. In contrast, Aerobic Efficiency (EF) measures external mechanical output (speed) normalized by internal biological load (heart rate), revealing the runner's true underlying cardiorespiratory engine unmasked by environmental noise.
Best of all, requiring only [Distance, Time, Heart Rate], EF is computed 100% non-invasively from any modern GPS watch (Garmin, Apple Watch, COROS, Suunto) without needing expensive chest straps or specialized laboratory equipment.

1st Inflection Point: Aerobic Threshold (LT1 / VT1) EFFICIENCY PEAK

The physiological transition where carbohydrate glycogen recruitment begins alongside pure lipid oxidation (Zone 2). The plateau of peak aerobic efficiency begins a gentle downward taper, marking the fundamental baseline of your Marathon Race Pace (M Pace).

2nd Inflection Point: Lactate Threshold (LT2 / VT2) SHARP CLIFF DROP

The upper limit where blood lactate production outpaces cellular clearance (approx. 4.0 mmol/L OBLA). Heart rate spikes while velocity efficiency reaches physical limits, causing the EF curve to fall steeply off a cliff, strictly defining your true Threshold Tempo Pace (T Pace).

Physiological Adaptation: The "Right & Downward Shift" of the Lactate Curve PHYSIOLOGICAL ADAPTATION

With consistent aerobic base building and polarized threshold workouts, myocardial hypertrophy, capillary bed expansion, and mitochondrial density surge, shifting your metabolic curve rightward and downward.

LT1 Rightward Shift (Zone 2 Pace Expansion)
A novice runner who felt out of breath at 10:30/mi (6:30/km) will progressively cruise at 8:50/mi (5:30/km) or 8:00/mi (5:00/km) at the exact same Zone 2 heart rate on pure fat metabolism. Your conversational speed ceiling expands significantly.
LT2 Delay (Postponing Lactate Inundation)
Monocarboxylate transporters (MCT lactate shuttles) and intramuscular buffering clear lactate rapidly, pushing your breakdown threshold from 8:00/mi (5:00/km) back to 6:50/mi (4:15/km) or 6:10/mi (3:50/km). The wall of exhaustion is delayed to much faster velocities.
💡 The Engine Displacement Metaphor:
It is equivalent to upgrading from a 1.0L compact car screaming at 4,500 RPM to cruise at 60 mph to a 3.0L V6 hybrid purring effortlessly at 1,800 RPM at the same speed. At identical velocity, your heart rate drops from 165 bpm to 135 bpm, sending your EF score leaping from 1.15 to 1.40+.

4 Validation Criteria for Accurate Single-Session Threshold Detection

1. ⛰️ Flat Terrain Control (≤25 ft / 8m elevation gain per mile): Run on a standard 400m track, flat park path, or indoor treadmill at 1% incline (mimicking road air resistance). Hills depress EF on climbs and artificially inflate it on descents.
2. 🏃 Multi-Stage Progressive Build-up (20+ minutes): Accelerate gradually by 15-20 sec/mi (10-15 sec/km) every 3 to 5 minutes, independent of total distance.
3. 💓 Dynamic Heart Rate Range: Ensure your session spans from recovery/warm-up (Zone 2) through threshold (Zone 4) with a span of at least 25-30 bpm between min and max heart rate.
4. ⏱️ Steady Warm-up: 5 to 10 minutes of easy jogging before beginning progression prevents early cardiac drift distortion.

[Gold Standard] Karvonen HRR 99% Precision vs Empirical Estimation

PRECISION CALIBRATION

RunAnalyz provides a 2-Tier Threshold Diagnostic Engine. Smartwatch athletes (Garmin, Apple Watch, COROS, Suunto) can enter their watch-verified MHR (Max HR) and RHR (Resting HR) to obtain clinical-grade 99% precision thresholds. For users without pre-measured metrics, our engine automatically falls back to an empirical statistical estimate (~85% accuracy).

Karvonen HRR Precision Mode 99% Accuracy (Recommended)
  • Formula: Heart Rate Reserve ($HRR = MHR - RHR$) via Karvonen formula
  • LT1 (Aerobic Threshold): $RHR + \text{round}(HRR \times 0.69)$ (Matches Garmin Zone 2 ceiling 1:1)
  • LT2 (Lactate Threshold): $RHR + \text{round}(HRR \times 0.87)$ (Matches Garmin LT threshold 1:1)
  • Key Benefit: Maintains immutable biological baselines immune to single-day fatigue, hill elevation, or heat.
Empirical Estimation Mode ~85% Accuracy (Default)
  • Formula: Session average HR, peak HR, and Jack Daniels VDOT inversion
  • LT1 / LT2: Evaluates HR spread and EF slope across progression tiers
  • Requirements: Valid on flat terrain runs over 20 min with 3-stage progressive pace
  • Notice: On hilly routes or recovery jogs, enter your watch MHR/RHR for absolute fidelity.

VDOT 5 Training Pacing Zones & Cadence Essentials

  • Cadence (Recommended 170–185 spm): Steps per minute. Higher cadence reduces ground contact time and vertical oscillation, slashing impact forces on knees and Achilles tendons.
  • E Pace (Easy / Recovery): Zone 2 heart rate baseline. Builds mitochondrial density, heart stroke volume, and connective tissue resilience.
  • M Pace (Marathon Pace): Full marathon target pace near LT1 aerobic threshold, optimizing glycogen conservation and fat oxidation.
  • T Pace (Threshold / Tempo): The critical pace at lactate threshold (LT2, ~4.0 mmol/L). Trains your body to buffer and clear lactate at high velocities.
  • I Pace (Interval / VO2max): High-intensity bouts (3–5 minutes with equal jog recovery) targeting maximum oxygen uptake velocity ($v\text{VO}_2\text{max}$).
  • R Pace (Repetition): Short, explosive sprints (200m–400m with full recovery) sharpening neuromuscular mechanics and running economy.

Weekly Volume & 10% Injury Prevention Safety Rule

The greatest hazard for endurance runners is overuse injury (shin splints, IT band syndrome, plantar fasciitis) caused by sudden mileage spikes. RunAnalyz codifies the sports medicine Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio (ACWR) safety guidelines into real-time diagnostics.

⚠️ What is the 10% Rule (Rule of 10)?
Ensure your total running mileage for the current week does not exceed the previous week's total by more than 10%. Tendons and cartilage remodel much slower than skeletal muscle; controlled, gradual progressive overload is non-negotiable.

Status Badge Weekly Volume Δ Physiological Load & Actionable Coaching
🟢 SAFE ≤ +10% or Deload Optimal adaptation zone. Fitness compounds steadily with minimal musculoskeletal injury risk.
🟠 CAUTION +10% to +20% Cumulative muscle fatigue is elevated. Prioritize foam rolling, deep sleep, hydration, and nutritional intake.
🔴 DANGER > +20% Acute overtraining hazard. Deload the following week by 15-20% to facilitate biological tissue repair.
