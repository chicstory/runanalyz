PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

RunAnalyz and its companion service ShoeF treat your sensitive training and biometric data with utmost care, strictly adhering to international privacy principles (GDPR, CCPA).

Article 1

Core Principles & Zero-Server Architecture

RunAnalyz and ShoeF (the "Service") is a non-commercial indie engineering project born out of pure passion for endurance science. The Service is built upon an uncompromising "Zero-Server Privacy Principle": we collect, harvest, and store zero personal data on any remote database.

🔒 100% Client-Side Local Computation:
Garmin and Strava activity records, raw GPS tracks, and runner profile parameters linked to RunAnalyz are never sent to or retained on any remote server. All computations and visualizations take place exclusively inside your browser's private local memory sandbox (SessionStorage / LocalStorage / IndexedDB).
Our companion service ShoeF (Running Shoes Lab) is fully open without requiring sign-up or accounts. 1:1 comparison lists and runner shoutbox tips are cached locally and verified anonymously.

Article 2

Categories of Data Processed Locally

The following data categories are temporarily read or cached exclusively inside your browser environment:

  • Runner Profile Settings (Optional): Age, Gender, Max Heart Rate (MHR), and Resting Heart Rate (RHR) — Retained strictly in your browser's localStorage solely to compute Karvonen Heart Rate Reserve (HRR) thresholds and age-graded benchmarks. Never transmitted off-device.
  • Strava Activity Data: Workout timestamp, total distance (mi/km), moving time, calories, and unique activity ID
  • Pace & Biometric Streams: Split paces, average/max heart rate (bpm), and Aerobic Efficiency Factor (EF)
  • Running Dynamics (if recorded by watch): Average cadence (spm), stride length, and elevation gain
  • Geospatial Coordinates: GPS polylines for route visualization on your local running heatmap
  • Usage Telemetry: Anonymized visit logs, browser user-agent, and screen dimensions (via Google Analytics 4 for technical optimization)
Article 3

Purpose of Processing

Data processed within your browser is utilized solely to deliver meaningful endurance diagnostics to you:

  1. Diagnosing aerobic decoupling (Pw:HR drift) and Karvonen LT1/LT2 thresholds for individual sessions
  2. Benchmarking performance against global age/gender percentiles (The State of Running 2019 dataset)
  3. Auditing weekly mileage build-up and enforcing the 10% ACWR rule to prevent overuse injuries
  4. Generating monthly recap visuals and high-resolution Instagram Story share cards
  5. Archiving multi-year annual mileage and aerobic progression trends
  6. Rendering private, interactive GPS heatmaps of your outdoor routes
Article 4

Cookies and Google Analytics

To monitor uptime and optimize rendering performance, the Service utilizes Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-QREC5DVG9M) provided by Google LLC.

  • Google Analytics collects anonymized telemetry (pages viewed, session duration, device type) via first-party cookies.
  • You may block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings, or install the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
Article 5

User Rights, One-Click Disconnect & Permanent Purge

You retain unconditional sovereignty over your fitness data:

  • 1-Click Strava Disconnect: Clicking [Disconnect] in the top navigation or runner profile immediately and permanently purges your Strava OAuth tokens and all cached activities from your browser storage. Zero server trace remains.
  • Reset Profile: The [Reset to Defaults] button in your profile settings clears custom MHR, RHR, and age parameters with one tap.
  • Browser Storage Clear: Using your browser's "Clear Site Data" utility instantly wipes all stored data and returns the app to pristine demo state.
Article 6

Non-Commercial Commitment & Minimal Ad Policy

RunAnalyz is built for fellow runners, not commercial monetization. We reject interstitial pop-ups, video takeovers, and paywalled physiological metrics.

To offset ongoing domain registration and Strava API developer subscription overhead, we reserve the right to display at most a single static, non-intrusive banner or voluntary coffee tip link.
