RunAnalyz: Aerobic Efficiency (EF) Running Engine

Beyond raw distance and vanity splits. A lightweight sports science platform built to diagnose your true cardiovascular adaptation through Aerobic Efficiency Factor (EF) and biomechanical dynamics.

Our Mission & Philosophy

Every runner pushes their limits day after day. Yet most commercial running apps focus solely on external outputs — "how many miles did you log today?" or "how fast was that mile?"

RunAnalyz anchors its core diagnostic engine in the Aerobic Efficiency Factor (EF): the speed produced per beat of cardiac work. Running an identical 7:30/mi (or 4:40/km) pace at 145 bpm instead of 165 bpm represents authentic cardiovascular expansion and metabolic durability.

💡 Core Credo: "Heart over miles, efficiency over ego. Data-driven running science for sustainable, injury-free lifelong progression."

The Story Behind RunAnalyz: Born from Runner's Curiosity

"Is my daily training pace truly aligned with my physiological aerobic threshold?", "Where does my cardiac efficiency rank among fellow runners in my age category?"

As a dedicated distance runner, I constantly looked for clear visibility into aerobic decoupling (Pw:HR drift) and true EF progression across seasons. Existing commercial platforms either buried these vital metrics behind hefty monthly paywalls or left them unexplained.

Driven purely by an indie runner's obsession with data and clean UI, I set out to build RunAnalyz: an instant, open-access diagnostic engine for runners worldwide.

🔒 100% Zero-Server Local Privacy Principle:
RunAnalyz never stores your private activity records or athlete profile in a central database. When you link your Strava account, your workout data is read directly into your browser's private local memory (IndexedDB/localStorage) to render your analytics. The moment you disconnect, 100% of your cached data is permanently erased.

☕ Minimal Ad & Open Community Commitment:
RunAnalyz is a non-commercial indie engineering project. Beyond a single unobtrusive banner or voluntary coffee sponsorship to cover domain and Strava API maintenance fees, we pledge never to deploy intrusive pop-up ads or paywalled core features.
